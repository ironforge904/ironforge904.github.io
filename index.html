Iron sharpens iron.

Iron Forge Ministries is a group of Christians in Jacksonville who go where people are hurting. We pray with them and share a meal. Then we keep showing up, week after week, until strangers become friends.

“Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another.” Proverbs 27:17

Our mission

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you.”

Matthew 28:19–20

Jesus gave His followers this command, and it’s the reason we go out every week. We want everyone we meet to know Him and to have people who will walk alongside them as they follow Him.

A baptism in the back of the truck.

Every Monday at 6 PM.

We’ve gone out every Monday night since early 2026, and we haven’t stopped. You don’t need experience or an invitation. Anyone is more than welcome to come.

Inside the Salvation Army

Iron Forge has a contract with The Salvation Army in downtown Jacksonville. Inside the building we preach to the people staying there and lead Bible study groups.

The Salvation Army
900 West Adams Street
Jacksonville, Florida

Out on the streets

From there we take the ministry outside. At every stop we pray with people and get to know them by name. We hand out food and clothes. When someone is ready to work, we help them find a job.

  • Trinity Rescue Mission
  • The Jacksonville bus stop
  • Sulzbacher homeless shelter
